DATA ANALYST

Company: Business Intelligence Partners
Location: Chicago, IL

Job Description:
We are seeking a detail-oriented Data Analyst to join our analytics team. The successful candidate will be responsible for transforming data into insights that drive business decisions.

Responsibilities:
- Collect, process, and analyze large datasets
- Create interactive dashboards and reports
- Develop and maintain SQL queries for data extraction
- Identify trends, patterns, and insights from data
- Collaborate with business stakeholders to define requirements
- Ensure data quality and integrity
- Present findings to technical and non-technical audiences
- Support data-driven decision making across departments

Qualifications:
- 2+ years of experience in data analysis or business intelligence
- Strong proficiency in SQL and data visualization tools
- Experience with Python or R for data analysis
- Knowledge of statistical analysis techniques
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills
- Strong communication and presentation skills
- Attention to detail and accuracy

Technical Skills:
- SQL (Advanced queries, joins, aggregations)
- Data Visualization (Tableau, Power BI, or similar)
- Python (Pandas, NumPy) or R
- Excel (Advanced functions, pivot tables)
- Database management systems

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with cloud data platforms (Snowflake, BigQuery)
- Knowledge of ETL processes
- Understanding of business metrics and KPIs
- Experience in [specific industry] is a plus

Education:
- Bachelor's degree in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, or related field
